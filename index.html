THE ORIGIN

Where the Hills
Shape the Tea

High in the hills of eastern Nepal, Ilam's cool climate, mountain air and fertile landscapes create an exceptional environment for tea. ILAM MART celebrates the character of this place through carefully crafted Nepali tea.

Every cup begins with the people who care for the tea gardens. ILAM MART is rooted in the work of local growers and the knowledge carried through tea-making communities.
